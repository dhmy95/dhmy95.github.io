# Privacy policy

This policy covers the Google OAuth app "rclone" registered by the GitHub account dhmy95.

The app is used by one person only: its owner. It runs the open-source program rclone on the owner's own computer to copy the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

No data is collected, stored, shared or sold by this app or by this website. Access tokens stay on the owner's computer. Google user data is used only to perform the owner's own backups and is never transferred to any third party.
